# Milestone 41: The active roster

Status: planned. It fills out the CP unlock catalog the rules promise, so it is
Milestone 41 in the project sequence. It gives `learn` and the CP economy
something worth buying and turns two dormant seams, the table-driven unlock
dispatch and the condition framework, into a live roster of maneuvers, cyberware,
and spells.

## Goal

Section 11 of `chromesix.md` lists roughly two dozen combat maneuvers, cyberware
mods, and spells, each a permanent 5 CP unlock with a per-use Grit cost. The
engine holds four: `smartlink` (aim), `dermal`, `reflex`, and `vigor`. So the
M35 advancement loop earns CP and the M36 `learn` verb spends it, but there is
almost nothing on the shelf. A character who banks CP has three passive stat
mods and one active maneuver to choose from.

This milestone stocks the shelf. It adds a representative unlock for each effect
family the rules describe, so every kind of active option is proven end to end:
a timed self-buff, an offensive unlock that imposes a condition, and an aid
action that restores Body Points. Each is a catalog row plus, where a family is
new, one effect tag and the single site that reads it. It is deliberately not the
whole table. Once a family's seam is proven, the remaining options in that family
are follow-on rows, not new code, which is the whole point of the M36 design.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The unlock catalog is table data.** `cs_unlocks` in `chromesix_verb.h` holds
  each unlock as one row: id, CP cost, required hook, graft flag, per-use Grit,
  and an effect tag. `learn` reads the cost and gates; `use` reads the Grit cost
  and switches on the tag (`verb_use.c`). A new maneuver is a row plus, if its
  effect is new, one `switch` arm. `cs_has_unlock` tests ownership.
- **Passive effects read where the stat applies.** `cs_recalc` already folds the
  passive `dermal` (+2 Soak), `reflex` (+2 Passive Defense), and `vigor` (+3 Max
  Grit) unlocks into the derived block, gated on `cs_has_unlock`. A timed buff
  reads the same two places, gated on a live buff prop instead.
- **The condition framework.** M40 made imposing a condition a prop on the sheet
  read where it bites: the turn grant for action-denial, `cs_attack_resolve` for
  pool and Passive Defense shifts. Shaken already sits in `cs_attack_resolve` as a
  -1D attack modifier, the exact shape Suppressed follows.
- **The per-turn tick.** `cs_cond_tick` (M40c), called at the turn grant, applies
  Ongoing damage. It is the natural home for counting down a timed buff and
  expiring it, so buff duration costs one extension, not a new hook.
- **The inflictor pattern.** `verb_trip`, `verb_menace` (stun, menace, rend) each
  roll an opposed or attack check on your turn and set a condition on the target.
  An offensive unlock activated through `use` follows the same shape, reusing the
  attack targeting and the skill-check engine.
- **The heal shape.** `verb_treat.c` (M39b) rolls the better of Medicine or
  Cybertech against a target number and mends on success. An in-fight aid action
  is the same roll, restoring Body Points instead of clearing a wound.

## Design: rows on the shelf, effects on their seams

Every unlock in this milestone is a `cs_unlocks` row. A passive or timed stat mod
reads in `cs_recalc`; an active effect gets an effect tag that `verb_use`
switches on. A timed buff stores its remaining rounds in a prop that `cs_cond_tick`
counts down, and `cs_recalc` reads while it is live. A condition inflictor sets a
target prop that `cs_attack_resolve` or the turn grant reads, the M40 pattern. An
aid action rolls and writes Body Points. No new dispatch, no new file for the
maneuvers themselves; the offensive and aid unlocks may need a small verb if `use`
cannot carry a target, but that is decided per slice, not up front.

The hooks gate the roster as the rules intend: a spell row requires the
`awakened` hook (as `vigor` does), a cyberware row requires `cyber` and a free
graft slot (as `dermal` and `reflex` do), and a plain maneuver requires neither.

## Slice a: timed self-buffs

The first effect family: an activated unlock that raises a defense for a set
number of rounds, then lapses on its own. This extends `cs_cond_tick` from
damage only to also counting down and clearing timed effects, and teaches
`cs_recalc` to read a live buff.

- **Mana Shield** (spell, Awakened, 1 Grit): +3 Passive Defense while active. A
  round-counted buff prop, refreshed on use, read in `cs_recalc`.
- **Dermal Wire Mesh** (cyberware, cyber graft, 1 Grit + action): for 3 rounds add
  your Wit dice to your Soak TN. The same mechanism on Soak.

Shipped work: a `pd_buff` and `soak_buff` prop, each carrying rounds remaining;
new effect tags in the `verb_use` switch that set them; `cs_cond_tick` decrements
the live buffs at the turn grant and clears them at zero; `cs_recalc` adds the
buff to Passive Defense and Soak while it is live. Two catalog rows.

## Slice b: offensive unlocks and the Suppressed condition

The second family: an activated unlock that rolls against a foe and imposes a
condition, reusing the M40 inflictor pattern. It also lands the first of the
M40-deferred conditions, since its inflictor arrives here.

- **Static Shock** (spell, Awakened, 1 Grit + action): roll Wit + Spellcasting vs
  the target's Passive Defense for 4D damage; a Tactical Edge (a wild-die success)
  also leaves the target Stunned, reusing the M40b Stunned skip.
- **Suppressive Fire** (maneuver, 1 Grit + action): mark an arc so a foe acting in
  it before your next turn is Suppressed, -1D on attacks. Suppressed is the
  M40-deferred condition, a prop read in `cs_attack_resolve` exactly as Shaken is.

Shipped work: the Suppressed condition (a prop, its -1D fold in
`cs_attack_resolve`, its name in `cs_cond_list`); the two unlocks as catalog rows
with an effect that rolls the check, applies the damage or the mark, and sets the
condition on a win. Whether these dispatch through `use` with a target word or a
small companion verb (as `trip` and `rend` did) is settled in the slice.

## Slice c: the aid action

The third family: an activated unlock that restores Body Points to yourself or an
ally in reach, the in-fight counterpart to `verb_treat.c` and the first ally-
targeted effect in the roster.

- **Biomedical Injector** (cyberware, cyber graft, 1 Grit + action): roll Wit +
  Medicine vs Moderate (10), restore Body Points equal to the margin, capped at
  your Wit dice, to yourself or an ally in reach.

Shipped work: one catalog row and the aid effect, rolling the check and adding
Body Points to the chosen target up to its maximum. It exercises the ally-
targeting seam, so a later ally-facing unlock is a row, not new plumbing.

## Deferred: reactions and the long tail

The reaction-family unlocks (Riposte, Emergency Defibrillator, Reflex Governor,
Empathic Aegis) all fire out of turn on being hit or on an ally being hit, so they
share one new mechanism, a hook in the attack-and-damage path that spends the
reaction slot (`react_left`). They land together as a later reactions milestone
rather than scattered here. The remaining Section 11 rows (Wired Reflexes,
Tactical Co-Processor, Threat-Assessment Optics, the other spells and cyberware)
are follow-on rows once each family above is proven, and the M40-deferred Blinded
and Held conditions arrive with their inflictors the same way Suppressed does
here.

## Verification

A smoke test drives each unlock end to end. It grants the unlock to a test
character, confirms `learn` gates it on CP and the required hook, and then uses
it: the buff test reads the raised Passive Defense or Soak on the sheet while the
buff is live and confirms it lapses after its rounds; the offensive test lands the
condition on a built foe in an isolated room and confirms the Suppressed or
Stunned effect on the foe's next turn; the aid test restores Body Points to a
damaged ally. The combat parts re-send on a fast poll so an action lands inside
the turn window, and down the foe with a `fight_over` teardown, the established
pattern.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M35 (the CP economy and `learn`), M36 (the table-driven unlock
catalog and the `use` dispatch these rows plug into), M40 (the condition
framework and `cs_cond_tick`, which slice a extends and slice b reuses), and
M39b (`verb_treat.c`, the heal shape slice c follows).
