# Milestone 51: Arc and quest visibility

Status: planned. It makes the campaign state a player has earned legible in play, so it
is Milestone 51 in the project sequence. M37 built jobs, M47 turned them into a chain
gated by earned flags, but nothing shows a player what they are working on or what they
have unlocked. This milestone surfaces that state.

## Goal

A player earns campaign flags (M47) that gate later contracts, holds one active
contract at a time, and carries faction standing. The sheet already shows standing,
credits, CP, maneuvers, and conditions, but the flags are invisible everywhere, and the
active contract appears only as a bare name with an "in progress" or "done" marker.
A player who logs back in mid-arc cannot see what the current job asks, what it pays, or
which clearances they hold and therefore which contracts have opened to them.

This milestone makes that state visible. It renders the earned flags as clearances,
grows the active-contract readout into a full briefing, and adds one command that
consolidates the two with a standing summary into a single "what am I working on"
view. It surfaces state that already lives on the sheet and the giver objects, so it is
content-light in code and adds no new mechanics.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The sheet readout.** `verb_sheet.c` prints attributes, defense and soak, credits,
  stims, CP, load, conditions, maneuvers, skills, and faction standing (each `id:step`
  pair rendered as a band word by `cs_standing_word`). It reads only the caller's own
  sheet, so it is non-setuid.
- **The flags list.** M47 stores earned campaign flags as a comma-separated `flags`
  list on the sheet, read by `cs_has_flag` and written by `cs_flag_add`. Nothing
  displays it.
- **The active contract.** The sheet holds `job_giver` (the giver's id) and `job_done`.
  `verb_jobs.c`'s `jobs` view lists the offers in the room, then the active contract as
  its giver's name plus "(in progress)" or "(done; report back)".
- **The contract fields on a giver.** A giver carries `job_desc`, a goal (`job_target`
  bounty or `job_dest` courier), rewards (`job_creds`, `job_cp`, `job_standing`), and
  the M47 gate and grant (`job_need`, `job_grant`). `show_offer` already reads these to
  print an offer, marking it locked when a `job_min` or `job_need` is unmet.
- **The verb routing.** `jobs`, `accept`, `abandon`, `turnin`, and `report` are all
  verbs bound to `verb_jobs.c`, which routes on `vm_args->verb`. A new view verb slots
  in the same way.

## Design: surface earned state where a player looks for it

Three additions, each reading state that already exists.

The flags list becomes a "Clearances" line on the sheet, rendered like the maneuvers
line, so the tokens a player earned (the M47 flags are readable words) show up beside
their standing.

The active-contract readout grows from a name into a briefing: its objective (the
bounty target or the courier destination), its reward (credits, CP, a standing step,
and any clearance it grants), and where to report. This reads the giver object the
sheet already points to.

A new `contracts` verb consolidates the briefing, the clearances, and a standing
summary into one view a player can call from anywhere, so "what am I working on and
what have I unlocked" is one command, not a hunt across `jobs` and `sheet`. Like the
other own-sheet views, it is non-setuid.

## Slice a: clearances on the sheet

Make earned campaign flags legible.

- Read the sheet's `flags` list in `verb_sheet.c` and print a "Clearances:" line when
  it is non-empty, in the shape of the existing maneuvers line.

A smoke test earns a flag (a granted M47 contract, or a direct set on a non-admin
sheet) and confirms the flag appears under Clearances on that player's `sheet`, and
that a sheet with no flags prints no Clearances line.

## Slice b: the active contract in full

Turn the bare active-contract line into a briefing.

- In the `jobs` active-contract section, read the giver the sheet points to and print
  its objective (bounty target or courier destination), its reward (credits, CP,
  standing step, and any `job_grant` clearance), and the report-back giver, alongside
  the existing progress marker.

A smoke test accepts a seeded contract and confirms `jobs` shows the objective and the
reward for the active contract, not just its name and status.

## Slice c: a consolidated `contracts` view

One command for the whole picture.

- Add a `contracts` verb (routed through `verb_jobs.c`) that prints the active
  contract briefing from slice b, the clearances from slice a, and a one-line standing
  summary, reading only the caller's own sheet and the giver it references.

A smoke test drives a non-admin player through a step of the seeded arc and confirms
`contracts` shows the active briefing, the clearances earned so far, and the standing
summary in one view, and that a player with no active contract sees a clear "no active
contract" line with their clearances and standing still shown.

## Deferred: labels, area quest boards, and objective tracking

A registry that maps a flag token to a prose label, an area "quest board" that lists
every contract in a district and its prerequisites, and live objective tracking (a
progress counter toward a bounty or a breadcrumb to a destination) stay deferred as
later content. The tokens are already readable words, so a label registry is polish,
not a need, and the board and tracker are new views rather than surfacing existing
state, which is this milestone's scope.

## Verification

A smoke test confirms clearances appear on the sheet, the active contract shows its
objective and reward in `jobs`, and the new `contracts` view consolidates the briefing,
clearances, and standing, including the empty case. It is driven on a non-admin
character, since these are own-sheet views a player uses and admin does not change what
they read.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M25a (the sheet and `cs_derived` readout this extends), M37 (jobs, the
active-contract state, and the giver fields the briefing reads), M38 (faction standing
and `cs_standing_word`, the summary this surfaces), and M47 (the campaign flags this
renders as clearances).
