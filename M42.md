# Milestone 42: Reactions

Status: planned. It adds the out-of-turn reaction unlocks the combat rules
describe, so it is Milestone 42 in the project sequence. It builds one shared
reaction hook inside the attack resolution and lands the reaction family M41
earmarked for later, largely closing out the ChromeSix combat engine.

## Goal

Section 11 of `chromesix.md` lists four reaction unlocks: Riposte, Reflex
Governor, Emergency Defibrillator, and Empathic Aegis. Each fires out of turn when
a hit lands, so unlike the M41 unlocks they are not activated on your own turn
through `use`. M41 shipped the roster's other three families and deferred these,
noting that they share one new mechanism and belong in their own milestone.

This milestone builds that mechanism, a defender-reaction check inside
`cs_attack_resolve`, and adds the four unlocks, each a catalog row plus a check at
one point in the resolution. It is the last major combat family in Section 11, so
it substantially completes the combat engine. It also rounds out the cheap passive
rows so the catalog is close to whole.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The reaction slot.** `react_left` is refreshed at each turn grant (2 for an
  elite, 1 otherwise) in `verb_combat.c`. It is already spent by the readied
  `guard` (+2 Passive Defense, in `cs_attack_resolve`) and by `cs_free_strike`
  (the leaving-melee reaction), so a per-round out-of-turn budget exists.
- **The recursion guard.** `cs_attack_resolve` takes `allow_react`.
  `cs_free_strike` and the ambush strike pass `0`, so a reaction's own strike
  cannot trigger nested reactions. Every reaction in this milestone fires only
  when `allow_react` is set, and any counter-strike it makes passes `0`.
- **The guard precedent.** The `allow_react` block in `cs_attack_resolve` already
  reads the defender's stance and reaction slot and adjusts the hit before it
  lands. The new reactions hook the same resolution at different points.
- **The passive-read pattern.** `dermal` and `reflex` are `UEF_PASSIVE` rows that
  `cs_recalc` reads by id. A reaction unlock follows the same shape: a
  `UEF_PASSIVE` row, so `use` refuses it, read by id inside `cs_attack_resolve`,
  gated on `cs_has_unlock` plus a spare `react_left` plus its Grit cost. No new
  dispatch and no `use` target.
- **The strike shape.** `cs_free_strike` is the template for a reaction that
  strikes back: check the slot, spend it, call `cs_attack_resolve` with
  `allow_react` 0, and broadcast.

## Design: a defender-reaction hook in the resolution

A reaction is a `UEF_PASSIVE` catalog row that `cs_attack_resolve` reads at the
point it bites. When `allow_react` is set and the defender holds the unlock, has a
reaction left, and can pay its Grit, the reaction fires, spends the slot, and
annotates the attack line. The hook points are the miss margin (for Riposte), the
Net Damage (for Reflex Governor), and the lethal drop to 0 Body Points (for
Emergency Defibrillator). Empathic Aegis is the one third-party reaction: it reads
from a bystanding ally, not the defender, so it needs a scan of the room's
combatants for a conscious holder with a reaction to spend, reusing the
containment iteration the header already uses.

Because these read inside `cs_attack_resolve`, they mutate the sheets the same way
the guard block and the damage path already do (spend Grit and `react_left`, set a
burnout flag), which keeps the reaction logic in the one place a hit is resolved.

## Slice a: the hook and Reflex Governor (shipped)

Establish the pattern at the Net Damage point, the simplest hook because it only
adjusts a number already in hand.

- **Reflex Governor** (cyberware, cyber graft, 2 Grit + reaction): when the
  defender is hit, roll Wit + Cybertech vs the attack total; on a success, reduce
  that hit's Net Damage by the defender's Wit dice.

Shipped: one `cs_unlocks` row (`governor`, cyber graft, `UEF_PASSIVE` so `use`
refuses it) and the reaction check in `cs_attack_resolve` just after Net Damage is
computed. When `allow_react` is set, the defender holds `governor`, has a spare
`react_left`, and can pay 2 Grit, it spends both, rolls Wit + Cybertech against the
attack total, and on a roll that matches or beats it cuts the hit by the
defender's Wit dice, re-clamping at zero and annotating the line `[governor -N]`
(or `[governor failed]`). It reads only when reactions are allowed, so a
reaction's own strike cannot trigger it. A smoke test gives the player the unlock
and a high Cybertech pool, sends a high-Might foe against a zeroed Soak so the hit
always lands, drives the fight with holds so the foe's attack comes promptly, and
confirms the reduction annotation. This proves the "read a reaction unlock inside
the resolution, spend the slot and Grit, adjust the hit, annotate" seam the next
two reactions reuse.

## Slice b: Riposte and Emergency Defibrillator

Two more hooks at the other resolution points.

- **Riposte** (maneuver, 1 Grit + reaction): when an attacker misses the
  defender's Passive Defense by 4 or more, the defender spends a reaction to make
  an immediate free strike for base weapon damage. It hooks the miss path and
  reuses the `cs_free_strike` shape, striking with `allow_react` 0 so it cannot
  recurse.
- **Emergency Defibrillator** (cyberware, cyber graft, reaction): when a hit would
  drop the defender to 0 Body Points, it drops to 1 instead and sets a burnout
  flag, so it fires once until a downtime Hard (14) Wit + Cybertech repair clears
  it. It hooks the lethal-drop path and needs no Grit, only the intact mod.

## Slice c: Empathic Aegis and rounding out the catalog

The one third-party reaction, plus the cheap passive rows that finish the roster.

- **Empathic Aegis** (spell, Awakened, 1 Grit + reaction): when a defender is hit,
  a bystanding ally holding the spell may spend a reaction to add its Charm dice
  to the defender's Soak for that hit. It scans the room's combatants for a
  conscious holder with a reaction to spend, the first bystander-reaction path.
- **Kinetic Absorbers** and the other remaining passive or cyber rows (a permanent
  Passive Defense or stat adjustment read where its stat applies), so the Section
  11 catalog is substantially complete.

## Deferred: the initiative and marking unlocks

Wired Reflexes reorders the turn, and Tactical Co-Processor and Threat-Assessment
Optics debuff or mark a target on the actor's own turn, which is the M41b
offensive pattern rather than a reaction. They land as follow-on rows once
reactions are proven. The M40-deferred conditions (Blinded, Held, and a persistent
Exposed) stay separate, each a prop plus a hook in `cs_attack_resolve` or the
movement path.

## Verification

A smoke test drives each reaction end to end against a built foe in an isolated
room, the established pattern. It grants the unlock and stacks the pools so the
outcome is deterministic: it confirms Reflex Governor cuts a hit's damage, that
Riposte fires a free strike when the foe misses by a wide margin, that Emergency
Defibrillator leaves a lethally hit character at 1 Body Point and then does not
fire again until its burnout clears, and that a bystanding ally's Empathic Aegis
raises the defender's Soak. The combat parts re-send on a fast poll so an action
lands inside the turn window, and down the foe with a `fight_over` teardown.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M25c (the attack verb, `cs_attack_resolve`, and the sheet these
reactions read and write), M25d (the Grit gauge and the `react_left` reaction slot
the reactions spend), M35 and M36 (the CP economy and the table-driven unlock
catalog these rows join), and M41 (the roster's other families and the
`UEF_PASSIVE` read pattern the reaction unlocks follow).
