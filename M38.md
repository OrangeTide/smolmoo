# Milestone 38: Standing with teeth

Status: in progress. It gives faction standing real consequences beyond price,
so it is Milestone 38 in the project sequence. Slice a (access gating) and slice
b (hostility) have shipped; slice c (per-NPC disposition) is planned.

## Goal

M37 lets a player earn faction standing by finishing jobs, but standing barely
does anything with it. The only consequence today is vendor pricing:
`verb_store.c` shifts a price ten percent per step and refuses a sale at Hostile
or worse. The rules promise more. `chromesix.md` Section 14, "What Standing
Does", names three consequences, and only pricing is built.

This milestone builds the other two, so the standing a player grinds up in jobs
changes what the world lets them do and how it treats them. It closes the loop
M37 opened: the reward chain becomes job, then standing, then access and safety,
instead of job, then standing, then almost nothing. It ties together three
systems that already exist, jobs, combat, and vendors, and leans on helpers that
landed with M37.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The standing helpers.** `cs_standing` reads a faction step from the
  `standing` prop, `cs_standing_add` (M37) writes one clamped to the [-3, +3]
  band, and `cs_standing_word` names the band. Reading a standing to gate on is
  one call.
- **Pricing already gates.** `verb_store.c` reads the store's `faction`, looks
  up the buyer's standing, prices each item by it, and refuses the sale at step
  -2 or worse with the band word. This is the pattern the other consequences
  follow.
- **The reactive-mob hook.** `mob_behavior.h` holds `mob_react`, which the
  on-enter verb (`verb_mob.c`) and the mob agent (`agent_mob.c`) both call when a
  player enters. It reads the mob's comma-separated `behavior` list and greets or
  opens a fight (`aggro`). The aggro branch already builds a roster and spawns
  `__combat`, so a standing-driven attack reuses it rather than writing new
  combat setup.
- **The faction property convention.** NPCs and stores carry a `faction` prop
  naming the faction they answer to. A job giver is a builder object with its own
  props, so a gate or an offset is one more prop, not a new object kind.

## Slice a: Access gating (shipped)

The best contracts are closed to strangers (Section 14).

- **The gate.** A job giver carries an optional `job_min`, a "faction:step" like
  the `job_standing` reward M37 already parses. A player whose standing with that
  faction is below the step cannot take the contract.
- **The verbs.** `jobs` still lists a gated offer but marks it as locked with the
  required band, so a player sees what they are working toward. `accept` refuses
  a contract the player does not rank for, naming the band needed. Both reuse
  `cs_standing` and the same "faction:step" parser `turnin` uses, so the check is
  small and shares code with M37.

Shipped: `verb_jobs.c` gained a shared `job_fac_step` helper that parses a
"faction:step" pair, which `turnin`'s standing reward now also uses in place of
its inline parse. `accept` reads the giver's `job_min` and refuses when the
caller's `cs_standing` is below the step, naming the band; `show_offer` appends a
"(locked: needs <faction> <band>)" marker to a gated offer the caller cannot
take. A giver with no `job_min` is unchanged. A smoke test builds a gated giver,
confirms `jobs` marks it locked and `accept` refuses it at Neutral, then raises
the standing and confirms the same contract accepts.

## Slice b: Hostility (shipped)

At -2 a faction's people move against you when the odds favor them (Section 14).

- **The trigger.** `mob_react` gains a standing check: a mob with a `faction`
  set, meeting a player whose standing with that faction is Hostile (-2) or
  worse, aggros on entry even without an explicit `aggro` behavior token. A mob
  with no faction, or a friendlier newcomer, is unchanged, so existing mobs keep
  their scripted behavior.
- **Reuse.** The aggro path in `mob_react` already opens the fight, so the change
  is the entry condition, not new combat code. The vendor refusal at Hostile
  (already shipped) is the peaceful half of the same rule, so hostility now has
  both a social and a violent face.

Shipped: `mob_behavior.h` computes the aggro condition as the existing `aggro`
token or a hostile faction, so `mob_react` opens a fight when a mob with a
`faction` meets a newcomer whose `cs_standing` with that faction is -2 or worse.
Both the on-enter verb and the mob agent get it, since they share `mob_react`. A
smoke test enters a faction mob's room in good standing (greet only), then drops
to Hostile and re-enters; the fresh combat proves the aggro fires on standing
alone, and that the friendly entry did not open a fight.

## Slice c: Per-NPC disposition

A specific NPC starts at their faction's standing and carries a personal
modifier (Section 14), so a corporate fixer whose faction hunts you may still
deal with you because you have done right by them before.

- **The helper.** `cs_disposition(npc, sheet)` returns the effective standing an
  NPC holds toward a player: the player's standing with the NPC's `faction` plus
  the NPC's optional `disp` offset, clamped to the [-3, +3] band. It is the read
  the store, the job gate, and the aggro trigger all switch to, so one NPC can be
  friendlier or colder than its faction.
- **Scope.** `disp` is a plain signed int on the NPC, 0 by default, so an NPC
  with no offset behaves exactly as slices a and b left it. The change is one
  helper plus swapping three raw `cs_standing` reads for it.

## Deferred: standing decay and area gates

Standing that drifts back toward Neutral over time, and whole rooms or exits
gated by standing, are left for later. Decay needs a clock, and an exit gate
duplicates the access check on a different object; neither is needed to make
standing matter, which the three slices above already do.

## Verification

A smoke test, run as the non-admin `TestPlayer3` so the checks are not masked by
admin authority, sets a giver's `job_min` above the player's standing and
confirms `jobs` marks it locked and `accept` refuses it, then raises the
player's standing (with `cs_standing_add` through a turn-in, or a direct set)
and confirms the same contract now accepts. It sets a mob's `faction` and drives
the player's standing to Hostile, then confirms entering the room opens a fight,
and confirms a friendlier standing does not. It sets an NPC `disp` that lifts a
hunted player back into dealing range and confirms the store sells to them where
the raw faction standing alone would refuse.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M37 (the `cs_standing_add` writer and the "faction:step" parser this
reuses, and the job giver these gates attach to), M25f (faction standing and the
`cs_standing` read, and vendor pricing, the consequence this extends), and
OLC-4 (`mob_react`, the reactive hook slice b drives off standing).
