# Milestone 53: Combat condition lifecycle

Status: shipped (slices a, b, c; 6cb6077, 91e35b3, 08bd7f3). It closes three defects the
M33-M52 review found in how combat conditions begin, end, and roll, so it is Milestone 53 in
the project sequence. M40 built the condition
set (Prone, Stunned, Shaken, Ongoing damage), M42 and M45 added more (Suppressed, Exposed,
Marked, Blinded, Held), but nothing tears them down when a scene ends, one condition was only
half built, and a shared dice helper can hang on a malformed pool. This milestone makes the
condition set consistent from application to teardown.

## Goal

A combat condition should apply for the fight it belongs to and end with it. Three gaps break
that. First, the scene teardown in `verb_combat.c` clears the per-fight bookkeeping (cover,
moved, stance, reactions, band, surprise) but not the conditions themselves, so a combatant
that ends a fight Prone, Stunned, bleeding, Exposed, or Marked carries that state into its
next scene. Exposed and Marked have no clear path anywhere, so on a reused NPC they are
permanent. Second, Shaken applies its -1D attack penalty but not the other half of its rule,
that a Shaken combatant cannot advance on the source of its fear. Third, `cs_roll` turns a
negative dice pool into a huge positive one through the unsigned cast in `cs_divmod`, so a
caller that passes a negative pool spins the roll for billions of iterations.

This milestone gives every condition a defined end. It clears the conditions at scene
teardown, implements Shaken's advance-block, and hardens the roll against a negative pool so
no caller can hang it. It adds no new conditions or maneuvers; it makes the ones that already
exist behave to their own rules.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The condition set.** Conditions live as integer props on a combatant's sheet: `prone`,
  `stunned`, `shaken`, `bleed` (M40); `suppress` (M41b); `exposed`, `marked_by` (M45b);
  `blind`, `held` (M45c). `cs_attack_resolve` and the movement verbs read them, `cs_cond_tick`
  counts down the timed ones (`blind`, and the `shield`/`mesh` buffs) at each combatant's
  turn, and `cs_rally` clears `shaken` at end of turn.
- **The scene teardown.** When the last foe is down or the player yields, `verb_combat.c`
  runs a teardown loop over the combatants that resets the per-fight props: `cover_bonus`,
  `moved`, `stance`, `react_left`, `surprised`, `move_bank`, and the yield flags. The
  condition props are not in that list.
- **Where Shaken is applied.** The `menace` maneuver (`verb_menace.c`) sets `shaken` on its
  target. Nothing records who caused the fear, so nothing can block advancing on that specific
  foe.
- **The movement verbs.** `close` and `engage` share one path in `verb_move.c`: resolve the
  target foe (the named one for `engage`, the nearest live one for `close`), then reduce that
  foe's `band`. A `held` combatant is already refused there before any band change, which is
  the model for a second gate.
- **The dice roll.** `cs_roll(points, wild)` converts a pool to dice through
  `cs_divmod(points, 3, ...)`, which casts to `unsigned`. Its `dice > 0 ? dice : 1` guard
  catches a zero pool but not a negative one, since the unsigned cast makes a negative pool a
  large positive dice count.

## Design

Three independent fixes, one per gap, each small and local. Conditions gain a single clearing
helper so teardown and any future reset agree on the set. Shaken records its source when
applied and reads it back in the movement gate, then clears the source wherever it clears the
condition. The roll clamps its own input so the fix protects every caller, not just the ones a
review happened to find.

## Slice a: clear conditions at scene teardown

Give every condition an end when the fight does.

- Add `cs_clear_conditions(sheet)` to `chromesix_verb.h`, clearing `prone`, `stunned`,
  `shaken`, `bleed`, `suppress`, `blind`, `exposed`, `marked_by`, and the `fear_source` that
  slice b adds.
- Call it for each combatant in the `verb_combat.c` teardown loop, beside the existing
  per-fight resets.
- Leave the timed self-buffs (`shield`, `mesh`) to their `cs_cond_tick` countdown, so a buff a
  player paid for is not cut short by the fight ending.

A smoke test marks and exposes a reused NPC, ends the fight, opens a fresh scene with it, and
confirms the debuffs are gone, where today they would persist.

## Slice b: Shaken blocks advancing on the fear source

Finish the condition to its rule.

- When `menace` sets `shaken` on a target, record `fear_source` on that target as the
  menacer's sheet id (`verb_menace.c`).
- In the shared `close`/`engage` path in `verb_move.c`, after the target foe is resolved and
  before its band changes, refuse the move when the mover is `shaken` and the target's sheet
  is its `fear_source`. Advancing on any other foe stays allowed, as do retreat and
  disengage.
- Clear `fear_source` wherever `shaken` clears: `cs_rally` and slice a's teardown.

A smoke test menaces a foe into Shaken, confirms `close`/`engage` onto that foe is refused
while advancing on a second foe is allowed, then rallies and confirms the block lifts.

## Slice c: a defensive clamp on the dice roll

Stop a malformed pool from hanging the roll.

- Clamp the input at the top of `cs_roll`: a pool below zero becomes zero, so `cs_divmod`
  never sees a negative numerator and the dice count stays sane.
- This protects every caller. The per-site clamp already added to `verb_treat` becomes
  redundant but harmless, and the same latent hang in `verb_check` (outside this milestone's
  feature scope) is closed as a side effect.

A smoke test drives a check with a forced-negative pool (a Crashed, low-skill character) and
confirms it resolves to a roll instead of hanging.

## Deferred

A general condition-duration system, each condition counting down its own rounds rather than
clearing at teardown or on a maneuver, stays deferred. The conditions today are either
per-turn, cleared by a specific verb, or cleared at scene end, which covers play; a uniform
timer is a larger mechanic than this milestone's scope. Fear that persists across scenes, or a
morale system richer than the single Shaken step, is content, not this fix.

## Verification

A smoke test confirms conditions clear at scene teardown so a reused NPC starts clean, Shaken
refuses an advance on its source while allowing other movement and lifting on rally, and a
negative dice pool resolves instead of hanging. The three fixes are independent, so each
slice's test stands alone. Combat-scene tests follow the turn-loop pattern, polling for the
settled scene before the next command. The suite grows by the new checks; the milestone
changes condition lifecycle, not the combat maths.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M40 (the condition set, `cs_cond_tick`, and `cs_rally` this extends), M42 and M45
(the later conditions teardown must clear), and the movement model in `verb_move.c` and
`verb_combat.c` that `close`/`engage` and the teardown loop already use. Slice c depends only
on `cs_roll` and `cs_divmod` (M25a).
