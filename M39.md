# Milestone 39: In-game time and recovery

Status: planned. It gives a character who survives a fight a way to mend, and
adds the clock the world has been missing, so it is Milestone 39 in the project
sequence. Slice a (short rest), slice b (wound treatment), and slice c (the
clock's deferred consumers) are planned.

## Goal

M25i built the death side of recovery: a fallen character is inert, the paid
`recover` command and a tunable death clock both revive it at the recovery point
with BP and Wounds restored, and an ally can carry and `revive` a body in place.
What is missing is recovery for a character who lives. A fighter who walks away
hurt has no way to heal short of dying first: `recover` refuses anyone who is not
dead, there is no short rest, and Wounds only clear through a full revive. The
smoke suite shows the gap, resetting `bp` on the sheet by hand after each fight.

This milestone adds recovery between fights, the counterpart to M25i's recovery
from death. It leans on one new primitive, an in-game clock the verbs can read,
and once that exists it also pays off the two clock-dependent items M37 and M38
deferred. So the milestone has a spine, `sys_now`, and three consumers.

## Background: what is already in place

- **Death recovery is done (M25i).** `recover` (paid, dead only), the
  `death_sweep_cb` clock (tunable via `SMOLMOO_DEATH_MS`, default 24h),
  `player_revive` at room #101 restoring BP and Wounds, and ally `carry`/`revive`
  all exist. This milestone complements that, it does not touch it.
- **The damage state.** BP and `maxbp` (`cs_recalc`), the `wounds` ladder (1, 2,
  3+, read by `cs_condition`), and the downed and dying track in `verb_combat.c`.
- **Anticipated schema.** `spawn_clean` already clears `bp`, `wounds`,
  `death_tick`, and `decay_tick` on a fresh instance. `death_tick` is M25i's death
  clock; `decay_tick` is reserved and unused, waiting for the standing decay in
  slice c.
- **A consumable recovery precedent.** Stims restore Grit (not BP), the Grit
  money sink, so the pattern for a bought recovery and a per-rest cap exists.
- **No clock for verbs.** Verbs have `sys_suspend` (a relative delay) and agents
  get `EV_TIMER` dwells, but nothing returns the current time, so a verb cannot
  measure elapsed rest, a cooldown, or a decay interval. Slice a adds that read.
- **No safe-room flag.** Rooms carry no rest marker yet. Slice a adds one.

## Slice a: Short rest (BP)

BP climbs back over time in a safe place, with no rest command (Section 8).

- **The time read.** Add `sys_now`, the next host syscall (27), returning the
  current time in seconds from the host clock. It is the smallest primitive that
  serves all three slices. A time in seconds lets a stored stamp be diffed against
  a later read, which is all recovery, a cooldown, and a decay interval need.
- **The safe flag.** A room carries an optional `safe` flag. Resting happens only
  while the character is in a safe room and not in a fight.
- **The recovery, computed lazily.** A `rest_since` stamp on the sheet marks when
  the current safe, calm stretch began. When the sheet is read (`sheet`, and the
  status bar), the elapsed time against `rest_since` restores BP pro-rated toward
  `maxbp` over a tunable rest period, so recovery just happens with no per-player
  timer and no running agent. Entering an unsafe room, or a fight starting, resets
  `rest_since`, so crossing into danger restarts the clock. The rest period is a
  tunable constant or `#0` prop, so a test rests in seconds, not hours. Grit
  trickles back on the same rest, more slowly.

## Slice b: Wound treatment

Wounds do not rest off, and a living character cannot use the death clinic. They
need care (Section 8).

- **The clinic.** A clinic is a builder object marked for treatment. A `treat`
  (or `mend`) verb there clears one `wounds` step for a creds fee plus a Medicine
  or Cybertech check against the wound, refusing when there is no wound, no
  clinic, or too few creds. This is the living-character counterpart to M25i's
  dead-only `recover`, and it is where those recovery skills earn their length and
  creds drain.
- **Reuse.** It reuses the existing skill-check engine and the creds field, so it
  is a fee, a roll, and a `wounds` decrement, not new machinery.

## Slice c: The clock's deferred consumers

Two small features that only waited on a clock, now that `sys_now` exists.

- **Job cooldown (M37 deferred).** A job giver can carry an optional `job_cd`, a
  cooldown in seconds. `turnin` stamps the sheet, and `accept` refuses the same
  giver again until the cooldown elapses, so a contract can be run repeatedly but
  not farmed back to back. This finishes the repeatability M37 left open.
- **Standing decay (M38 deferred).** Standing drifts one step toward Neutral after
  a long idle interval, tracked with the reserved `decay_tick` and computed lazily
  on a standing read, so a reputation earned or lost fades if it is not renewed.
  It reuses `cs_standing_add` and stays within the [-3, +3] band.

## Deferred: conditions

The temporary conditions beyond Wounds (Stunned and the rest of Section 8's
condition list) are left for later. They are combat-turn effects, not recovery,
and belong with a combat-conditions pass.

## Verification

A smoke test flags a room safe, damages a character's BP, rests for the tuned
period, and confirms BP climbs back toward its maximum, then confirms a step into
an unsafe room resets the clock so no recovery accrues. It wounds a living
character, confirms a rest does not clear the wound, then treats it at a clinic
and confirms the fee is spent, the check runs, and the wound step drops, plus the
refusals (no wound, no creds). It confirms a job with a cooldown is refused on an
immediate re-accept and allowed once the cooldown elapses, and that a standing
step decays toward Neutral after the idle interval. The rest, cooldown, and decay
periods are tuned short so the test does not wait on real time.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M25a (the sheet, `money`, and skill checks), M25c (BP, the Wound
ladder, and the dying track), M25i (the death-recovery side this complements, and
the `player_revive`/clinic-fee pattern the wound clinic mirrors), M37 (the job
giver the cooldown attaches to), M38 (`cs_standing`/`cs_standing_add` the decay
reuses), and OLC-2 (`spawn_clean`, whose reserved `decay_tick` the decay uses).
