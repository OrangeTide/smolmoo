# Milestone 54: Standing that decays in play

Status: shipped (slices a, b, c; 986b651, e0df0a1, 126bb63). It closes a defect the M33-M52
review found in faction standing decay, so it is Milestone 54 in the project sequence. M38 built standing with teeth (pricing, service, and
aggro shift with a player's reputation) and M39c added a decay so old reputation fades if it is
not renewed. But the decay only runs when a player opens their sheet or the contracts view, so
every consequence reads a stale value until then. This milestone makes the decay reach the
consequences it was built to soften.

## Goal

Faction standing decays toward neutral over idle time (M39c), so a grudge cools and a favor
fades if a player does nothing to keep them. The decay is applied by `cs_decay`, which reads
the elapsed time and rewrites the sheet's `standing` prop. Today `cs_decay` is called from only
two places, the `sheet` readout and the `contracts` view. Every consequence of standing, a
store's pricing and its service refusal, a faction mob's aggro, and a contract's minimum
standing gate, reads the raw `standing` prop without decaying it first. So a player whose
hostility should have cooled keeps getting attacked, a faded discount keeps applying, and a
gate keeps opening or closing on a stale value, until the player happens to look at their
sheet, which lazily materializes the decay.

This milestone makes standing decay in play, not only on view. The consequences already share
one reader, so the fix is a single point: fade idle standing at that reader, and every
consequence reflects real elapsed time. It adds no new standing mechanic; it makes the decay
M39c built actually take effect where it matters.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The decay.** `cs_decay(sheet)` reads `decay_tick` (the last settle time) and `decay_secs`
  (the period, from the system object), drifts each faction's step toward zero by the elapsed
  periods, and rewrites the `standing` prop and `decay_tick`. It is cheap and idempotent: the
  first call seeds `decay_tick`, and a later call within the same period returns at once
  without rewriting. It is currently called from `verb_sheet.c` and the `contracts` view in
  `verb_jobs.c`, each just before rendering a standing summary.
- **The consequence chokepoint.** Every consequence reads standing through one helper,
  `cs_disposition(npc, sheet, faction)`, which calls `cs_standing(sheet, faction)` for the
  player's step and adds the NPC's `disp` offset (a per-NPC grudge or favor). Its callers are
  the store's pricing and service refusal (`verb_store.c`), the contract minimum-standing gate
  in `show_offer` and `accept` (`verb_jobs.c`, which the in-room `jobs` and the district
  `board` both render), and the faction-mob aggro check in `mob_react` (`mob_behavior.h`). In
  every call the `sheet` argument is the acting player's own sheet.
- **The decay test.** M39c already drives `cs_decay` directly, setting a short `decay_secs` and
  over-shooting a whole band, then reading the faded value from the sheet. It is the model for
  a shortened-window test that observes a consequence instead of the sheet.

## Design: fade idle standing at the one reader every consequence shares

Because `cs_disposition` is the single seam through which the store, the job gate, the board,
and aggro all read standing, one change closes the whole gap: call `cs_decay(sheet)` at the top
of `cs_disposition`, before it reads `cs_standing`. Every disposition check then fades the
player's idle standing first, so a consequence reflects real elapsed time whether or not the
player has looked at their sheet. `cs_decay` is idempotent and cheap, so calling it on the hot
paths (aggro on each newcomer, pricing per store item) costs nothing once a period has settled.

The `sheet` and `contracts` views keep their own `cs_decay` calls, since they render standing
through `cs_standing_line` rather than `cs_disposition`; the calls are idempotent, so a view
that also triggers a disposition check decays only once. A dedicated `cs_standing` read-through
was considered and set aside: `cs_disposition` already wraps `cs_standing` for every
consequence, so decaying there is both smaller and exactly scoped to the consequences, without
touching the many display-only `cs_standing` reads.

## Slice a: decay-through the disposition reader, proven on the job gate

Make the fix and prove it on a standing gate.

- Call `cs_decay(sheet)` at the top of `cs_disposition` in `chromesix_verb.h`, before the
  `cs_standing` read, so every consequence fades idle standing first.
- A smoke test on non-admin TestPlayer3 sets a standing just at a contract's `job_min`, sets a
  short `decay_secs`, lets the window pass, and confirms the contract reads as locked in `jobs`
  or `board` without the player having opened their sheet first, where before the fix it stayed
  open on the stale value.

## Slice b: the store reflects decayed standing

Prove the same reader reaches pricing and service.

- No new production code: the store already prices and refuses through `cs_disposition`, so
  slice a covers it. This slice adds the coverage.
- A smoke test gives TestPlayer3 a standing that earns a discount (or clears a service gate),
  lets it decay past the threshold with no sheet view, and confirms the quoted price or the
  service response reflects the faded standing.

## Slice c: aggro cools with the grudge

Prove the highest-value consequence, hostility.

- No new production code: `mob_react` reads through `cs_disposition`, so slice a covers aggro
  too. This slice adds the coverage.
- A smoke test sets TestPlayer3 to a Hostile standing with a faction, confirms the faction mob
  aggros on entry, then lets the standing decay back above the aggro threshold and confirms a
  later entry no longer draws aggro, again without a sheet view materializing the decay.

## Deferred

A standing model richer than linear decay toward neutral (asymmetric cool-down, a floor a
grudge never fully forgets, or events that renew standing) stays deferred as content. This
milestone only routes the existing decay to the consequences; tuning the curve is a separate
question. A read-through on the raw `cs_standing` reader for display-only paths is not needed,
since the sheet and contracts views already decay before they render.

## Verification

A smoke test confirms a standing gate, a store price or service, and faction aggro each reflect
decayed standing after an idle window, without the player opening their sheet first, which is
the behavior M39c intended and M38 relied on. It is driven on non-admin TestPlayer3, since
these are player-facing consequences a gate or a mob applies and admin authority does not
change them. The change is one call at a shared reader, so the combat and jobs maths are
untouched; the suite grows by the new checks.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M38 (standing, `cs_disposition`, `cs_standing`, and the store, gate, and aggro
consequences this reaches), M39c (`cs_decay` and the `decay_tick`/`decay_secs` machinery it
routes), and M52 (the `board`, which renders the same gate through `show_offer`).
