# Milestone 40: Combat conditions

Status: in progress. It adds the tactical conditions the combat rules describe,
so it is Milestone 40 in the project sequence. Slice a (the framework and Prone)
has shipped; slice b (Stunned and Shaken) and slice c (Ongoing damage and Crash
on rest) are planned.

## Goal

The combat engine tracks hit-point damage, the Wound ladder, the dying track, and
the stim Crash, but not the temporary conditions the rules lay out in Section 8:
Stunned, Prone, Blinded, Shaken, Suppressed, Held, and Ongoing damage. Only Crash
is a full condition today. Exposed and Slowed exist, but only in passing (a
surprise strike, encumbrance), not as tracked states.

This milestone adds a condition framework and the highest-value conditions, so a
fight is more than trading damage: a character can be knocked down, stunned,
frightened, or set bleeding, and must act around it. It is also the natural home
for effects that maneuvers (M35, M36) and a later spell system apply, so it opens
more than it builds. And it closes a small M39 leftover, since Crash is a
condition that should clear on a full short rest.

## Background: what is already in place

- **Crash is a complete condition.** `cs_stim_use` stacks it and
  `cs_crash_penalty` applies a -3 (one die) pool penalty per stack, the pattern a
  condition follows: a prop on the sheet, read where it bites.
- **Transient precedents.** `verb_ambush.c` applies Exposed (-2 Passive Defense)
  for a surprise strike, and `verb_move.c` applies Slowed through encumbrance, so
  the effects exist but are not yet tracked states.
- **The start-of-turn hook.** `verb_combat.c` grants a turn at one point
  (`cb_turn`/`cb_acted`, near line 409), right where it already clears the
  per-turn transient flags (`cover_bonus`, `moved`) and, a few lines above,
  already skips the turn of a flat-footed combatant with a "loses the round"
  message. That skip is the exact model for Stunned, and that clear point is where
  turn-lapsing conditions end and Ongoing damage ticks.
- **The attack-resolution hook.** `cs_attack_resolve` reads both sheets and
  already takes a `pool_mod` and a `pd_bonus`, so a condition's effect on the
  attack pool or the defender's Passive Defense folds in there, read from the
  sheets, in one place.
- **The maneuver dispatch.** M36 made `use` table-driven with an effect tag per
  unlock, the seam a condition-inflicting maneuver plugs into.

## Design: conditions as sheet props

A condition is a prop on the creature's sheet, in the idiom of `downed`,
`wounds`, and `crash`: a flag (`prone`, `stunned`, `shaken`) or a value (`bleed`
for Ongoing damage). A small `cs_cond_tick(sheet)` helper, called at the
start-of-turn clear point, ends the conditions that lapse there and applies the
ones that tick. `cs_condition` (the sheet's status line today) grows to list the
active conditions. The combat effects read the props where they bite: the
turn grant for action-denial, `cs_attack_resolve` for pool and Passive Defense
shifts. Each condition needs an inflictor, so every slice pairs a condition with
a concrete way to impose it, reusing the existing attack targeting or the
maneuver table rather than inventing a new one.

## Slice a: The framework and Prone (shipped)

- **The framework.** The condition-as-prop convention and the condition list on
  the sheet (`cs_condition` grows to name active conditions). Prone is the first
  condition: while Prone your attacks take -1D, melee against you is +1D, and
  ranged against you is -1D, all folded into `cs_attack_resolve` from the two
  sheets. You stand with a Move, so Prone clears through a stand action, not a
  turn lapse, which is why the start-of-turn `cs_cond_tick` helper waits for slice
  b, where Stunned and Ongoing need it.

Shipped: `cs_cond_list(sheet, o)` in `chromesix_verb.h` names the active
conditions, and `verb_sheet.c` prints a `Conditions:` line when any are set.
`cs_attack_resolve` folds the Prone modifiers into the attack pool from both
sheets (a prone attacker at -1D; a prone defender +1D to melee, -1D to ranged)
and annotates the attack line with `(prone)` or `(target prone)`. `verb_trip.c`
(verb 467, setuid) is the inflictor: on your turn, an opposed Might-plus-Brawl
roll against an engaged foe's better of Might or Agility, knocking it Prone on a
win and ending your turn. `verb_stand.c` (verb 468, setuid) clears Prone, spending
the turn in a fight. A smoke test shows the condition on the sheet, clears it with
stand, trips a foe in an isolated fight, and confirms the tripped foe's next
attack is annotated Prone.
- **The inflictor.** A `trip` maneuver (or shove) that targets an engaged foe and
  on a successful contested roll knocks it Prone, reusing the attack verb's foe
  targeting and the skill-check engine.

## Slice b: Stunned and Shaken

- **Stunned.** You lose your next action, though a reaction is still allowed. It
  hooks the turn grant exactly as the flat-footed skip does: on your turn, if
  Stunned, announce it, clear the flag, and pass. Inflicted by a stunning maneuver
  or a heavy hit.
- **Shaken.** Your attacks and saves take -1D and you cannot advance on the source
  of your fear. It ends when you rally, an ally's Command or your own Wit roll
  against Moderate (10) at the end of your turn, so it does not lapse on its own.
  Inflicted by a Fear attack (rules Section, Fear Attacks), a creature's
  `fear` profile rolling against the target's resolve.

## Slice c: Ongoing damage, and Crash on rest

- **Ongoing.** Bleeding, burning, or corroding: a set amount of direct, un-soaked
  damage at the start of each of your turns until you spend an action or make a
  save to end it. This is the first per-turn tick effect, applied by
  `cs_cond_tick` before the turn is handed over. Inflicted by a weapon flagged to
  bleed, so an attack that lands can set `bleed` on the target.
- **Crash on rest.** The M39 leftover: Crash clears on a full short rest. `cs_rest`
  clears the `crash` stack when a rest completes to full, so the stim penalty is
  the recovery cost the rules intend, not a permanent mark.

## Deferred: the remaining conditions

Blinded, Suppressed, Held, and a persistent Exposed follow the same framework, a
prop plus a hook in `cs_attack_resolve` or the movement path, and land as
follow-ons once the framework and the inflictor patterns are proven. The Grit
trickle on a rest (deferred from M39) stays deferred; it is a recovery detail, not
a condition.

## Verification

A smoke test drives each condition end to end against a built foe in an isolated
room, so the turn loop is exercised without disturbing the shared combat blocks.
It trips a foe and confirms the Prone pool and Passive Defense shifts and that a
Move clears it; it stuns a combatant and confirms the turn is skipped; it lands a
Fear attack and confirms Shaken applies its penalty and a rally ends it; it sets
an Ongoing bleed and confirms the tick damage lands each turn until it is ended;
and it confirms a full short rest clears a Crash stack. The combat tests poll for
the scene outcome before the next command, the established pattern, so a settled
scene does not reopen.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M25c (the attack verb, `cs_attack_resolve`, BP, and the sheet these
conditions read and write), M25d (the Grit gauge, maneuvers, and the turn task
the conditions hook), M36 (the table-driven `use` dispatch a condition maneuver
plugs into), and M39 (`cs_rest`, which slice c extends to clear Crash).
