# Milestone 37: Jobs and contracts

Status: shipped. It gives the world a purpose loop, a contract to accept and a
reward for finishing it, so it is Milestone 37 in the project sequence. Slice a
(offer, accept, track), slice b (bounty completion and payout), and slice c (the
courier goal and standing reward) have all shipped.

## Goal

The character systems are complete: a player can fight, talk, travel, grow, and
now spend Character Points on a real roster of unlocks. What the world does not
have is a reason to do any of it. Combat pays CP only for sparring, and the creds
and standing currencies have almost nothing feeding them.

This milestone adds a directed loop. A job giver offers a contract; completing it
pays creds, CP, and standing. It ties together the systems that already exist,
travel by vehicle, combat, persuasion, and it feeds the currencies the growth and
shop loops consume. A job is data on an object, authored in-game like the rest of
the world since M33, not hardcoded content.

## Background: what is already in place

- **The reward fields.** `money` and `cp` are plain integer props on the sheet,
  read and written directly (the shop deducts `money`, `award_cp` adds `cp`).
  Paying a reward is a read and a write.
- **The scene-win hook.** `verb_combat.c` resolves a won scene at one point,
  `foes_up(...) == 0`, where `award_cp` already runs with the roster of defeated
  foes in hand. A bounty's completion can be checked at that same point.
- **Standing.** `cs_standing` reads a faction step from the `standing` prop, but
  there is no verb-side writer yet; the host `@standing` command is the only way
  a step changes. A standing reward needs a small merge helper.
- **No delivery primitive.** There is no real `give`; items move with
  `get`/`drop`/`put`. A courier goal works from arrival or a drop, not a handoff.
- **Builder-authored world.** M33 lets a builder create and program objects
  in-game. A job giver is one more such object, its contract in props, so jobs
  are authored, not compiled in.

## Slice a: Offer, accept, and track (shipped)

The bookkeeping half of the loop.

- **The job giver.** An object marked `job=1` carrying one contract in props: a
  `job_desc` line, the goal (a target or destination), and the reward
  (`job_cp`, `job_creds`, and later a standing step). A plain builder object, no
  new object kind.
- **The player's state.** Two props on the sheet: `job_giver`, the objref of the
  contract in progress, and `job_done`, its completion flag. One active job at a
  time keeps the first slice small.
- **The verbs.** `jobs` lists the contract offered here and your active one;
  `accept` takes the job here, storing `job_giver` and clearing `job_done`;
  `abandon` drops it. `accept` refuses when you already hold a job, so the state
  stays single.

Shipped: `verb_jobs.c` (verbs 461-463, non-setuid) dispatches `jobs`, `accept`,
and `abandon` off the verb name. `jobs` scans the room with `sys_next` for
`job=1` givers and prints each offer and the player's active contract; `accept`
stores the giver's id in `job_giver` and clears `job_done`; `abandon` clears
them. The giver id is a plain int on the sheet (0 for none), so no objref
plumbing is needed. A smoke test builds a giver and runs the offer, accept,
already-have-one refusal, and abandon paths.

## Slice b: Completion and payout (shipped)

Detect a finished contract and pay it.

- **Bounty first.** The first goal type is a bounty, defeat a named target,
  because the detection point already exists. At the `award_cp` win point, if the
  player holds a job and a defeated foe matches its target (a name or a `kind`
  tag), set `job_done` on the sheet. No new scan and no new hook, just one check
  where the roster is already in hand.
- **Turn in.** `turnin` (or `report`) at the giver checks `job_done`, pays
  `job_cp` and `job_creds` into the sheet's `cp` and `money`, announces the
  reward, and clears `job_giver` and `job_done`. Turning in an unfinished or
  absent job is refused. Payout reuses the fields the shop and `award_cp` already
  use, so it is a read and two writes.

Shipped: `mark_job_done` in `verb_combat.c` runs at the same win point as
`award_cp`; if the player holds a job and a defeated foe's name matches the
giver's `job_target`, it sets `job_done`. `turnin`/`report` in `verb_jobs.c`
(verbs 464-465) requires the giver in the room and `job_done` set, then adds
`job_cp` and `job_creds` to the sheet's `cp` and `money` and clears the job. A
smoke test, run in a fresh room so the target resolves to the spawned raider and
not the lobby's downed proto, accepts a bounty, refuses an early turn-in, defeats
the target, confirms the job reads done, and confirms the turn-in pays out and is
then refused with no job.

## Slice c: The courier goal and a standing reward (shipped)

- **A courier goal.** A second goal type, reaching a destination, detected on
  arrival. The giver names the destination room in `job_dest`; `verb_go` sets
  `job_done` when the mover lands there, the travel-side mirror of the bounty's
  combat-side `mark_job_done`. Both goal types set the same flag, so `turnin` is
  unchanged. Report-back is at the giver, so a courier is a round trip.
- **A standing reward.** `cs_standing_add` (in `chromesix_verb.h`) is the
  verb-side writer that merges one `id:step` pair into the `standing` prop,
  clamped to the [-3, +3] band, the mirror of the read-only `cs_standing`. The
  giver names an optional `job_standing` "faction:step" that `turnin` pays, which
  the shop's standing-priced trade (rules Section 14) then feels.

Shipped: `verb_go.c` completes a courier on arrival at `job_dest`; `turnin` in
`verb_jobs.c` parses `job_standing` and calls `cs_standing_add`, announcing the
shift. A smoke test digs an origin and a destination, accepts a courier with a
standing reward, walks to the destination (completing it), reports back, and
confirms the payout line and the new faction band on the sheet.

## Deferred: repeatability

An optional per-job cooldown, so a contract can be run again without being
farmed, in the same spirit as the once-per-scene CP award, is left for later. It
needs a clock on the giver, and the single-job-at-a-time bookkeeping already
keeps a contract from being repeated within one run.

## Verification

A smoke test builds a job giver with a bounty contract and a target creature,
accepts the job, confirms `jobs` shows it active, defeats the target, and
confirms the win marks it done and `turnin` pays the creds and CP into the sheet
(read back as a balance change). It also confirms the refusals: accepting a
second job, turning in an unfinished one, and turning in with no job. The courier
and standing paths get their own checks when slice c lands.

## Dependencies

Depends on: M35a (the `award_cp` scene-win hook this reuses for bounty
completion), M25a/c (the sheet, `money`, and combat), and M33 (the in-game
authoring that makes a job giver a builder object, not compiled content).
